# Patch — All Gas build

## What Patch does

Something broke at home? Tell Patch. It finds a small number of people who publicly say they handle that kind of repair, asks them for their real price and time, brings their replies back into one place, and lets the user choose.

## Demo journey

`describe the repair → find relevant people → ask for price/time → receive real replies → choose someone`

## Sponsor responsibilities

- Convex: source of truth and live updates
- Firecrawl: source-backed public service evidence
- AgentMail: ordinary outbound/inbound email
- OpenAI: conservative interpretation of repair and reply facts

## Truth boundary

A public website may show that someone handles a repair. Only a real reply may establish current availability, price, timing, or acceptance.
